# Broad Market Index Funds (Nifty 50)

## What is an Index Fund?
An index fund is a passive mutual fund designed to mirror the composition and performance of a specific financial market benchmark, such as the **Nifty 50** (the 50 largest blue-chip companies in India) or the **BSE Sensex**.

## Why Choose Index Funds over Actively Managed Funds?
1. **Low Expense Ratio**: Direct index funds typically charge 0.10% - 0.20% per year, whereas active funds often charge 0.80% - 1.50%. This fee gap compounds into massive savings over 15-20 years.
2. **Zero Fund Manager Risk**: Active managers frequently underperform broad benchmark indices over rolling 5-10 year horizons (documented by S&P SPIVA India scorecards).
3. **Simplicity & Broad Diversification**: Automatically diversifies your money across top sectors: banking, IT, energy, consumer staples, automobiles, and pharmaceuticals.

## Best Practices:
- Keep an investment horizon of **at least 5 to 7 years**.
- Set up an automated monthly SIP and avoid checking daily price swings.
- For horizons under 3 years, avoid pure index funds due to short-term drawdown risk.
