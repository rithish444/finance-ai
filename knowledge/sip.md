# Systematic Investment Plan (SIP)

## What is a SIP?
A Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) is a method of investing a fixed sum of money at regular intervals (typically monthly) into a mutual fund scheme, rather than making a lump-sum commitment.

## Why SIP for Long-Term Wealth?
1. **Rupee-Cost Averaging**: When market prices drop (lower NAV), your fixed monthly amount buys more units. When markets rise, you buy fewer units. Over 5+ years, this automatically lowers your average cost per unit without timing the market.
2. **Power of Compounding**: Regular earnings generate their own earnings over decades. A ₹5,000 monthly SIP at 12% annual return can grow to over ₹50 lakhs in 20 years on an invested capital of just ₹12 lakhs.
3. **Behavioral Discipline**: Automated debits eliminate emotional decision-making, greed, and panic selling.

## Golden Rules for Indian Beginners:
- Match the SIP debit date to 2-3 days after your monthly salary is credited.
- Never pause an index SIP during market corrections; that is precisely when you accumulate cheaper units.
- Keep equity SIPs for horizons strictly greater than 5 years.
