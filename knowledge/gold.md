# Gold Allocation in Indian Personal Finance

## Why Hold Gold?
In Indian households, gold carries profound cultural value. Financially, its primary role is as a **crisis hedge and currency protector**, not a primary wealth creation compounding engine.

## Gold Vehicles Comparison:
1. **Physical Jewelry**:
   - Disadvantage: Incurs 10% to 25% making charges and GST (3%), plus locker/theft risk. Unsuitable for pure investment.
2. **Sovereign Gold Bonds (SGB - RBI issued)**:
   - Gold price appreciation linked directly to 999 purity gold.
   - 2.50% annual simple interest paid semi-annually into your bank account.
   - **100% Tax-Free capital gains** on 8-year maturity.
   - Unquestionably the most financially sound gold vehicle in India.
3. **Gold ETFs / Mutual Funds / Digital Gold**:
   - Backed by physical gold stored in vaults.
   - Highly liquid; tradeable during market hours starting from 1 unit (~0.01g).
   - Taxed according to standard debt asset capital gain rules.

## Golden Allocation Rule:
- Cap gold allocation strictly at **5% to 10%** of total net worth.
- Never prioritize gold purchases over paying off credit cards or building an emergency fund.
