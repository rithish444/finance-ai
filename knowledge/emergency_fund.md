# Emergency Safety Fund

## What is an Emergency Fund?
An emergency fund is a dedicated, liquid cash buffer set aside exclusively for unpredictable life contingencies:
- Sudden job loss or salary delay
- Medical emergencies not immediately covered by cashless insurance
- Critical home or vehicle repairs
- Family crisis assistance

## Sizing Formula:
- **Baseline (Single / Stable job)**: 6 months of essential survival expenses.
- **Family with Dependents**: 9 months of essential survival expenses.
- **Self-Employed / Variable Income / Business**: 12 months of essential expenses.

*Essential monthly survival expenses = Fixed obligations (Rent, EMIs, Utilities, School fees) + 50% of variable living expenses.*

## Where to Keep Emergency Funds:
- **Tier 1 (Instant 24/7 access, 1-2 months)**: Primary savings account with auto-sweep FD facility.
- **Tier 2 (Same-day / T+1 access, remaining months)**: Liquid mutual funds (instant redemption up to ₹50,000/day) or short bank FDs.

## Rules:
1. Never invest emergency money in the stock market or lock it in 15-year schemes like PPF.
2. Replenish immediately after any drawdown before resuming discretionary goal SIPs.
