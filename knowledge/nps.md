# National Pension System (NPS)

## What is NPS?
The National Pension System (NPS) is a voluntary, long-term, defined-contribution retirement savings scheme regulated by the PFRDA (Pension Fund Regulatory and Development Authority).

## Key Features:
1. **Ultra-Low Cost**: Fund management charges are among the lowest in the world (approx 0.03% to 0.09% p.a.), significantly lower than active mutual funds.
2. **Asset Allocation (Active vs Auto Choice)**:
   - Equity (E): Up to 75% equity allocation allowed.
   - Corporate Bonds (C) & Government Securities (G).
   - Alternative Assets (A): Up to 5%.
3. **Tax Advantages**:
   - Additional ₹50,000 exclusive deduction under Section 80CCD(1B) beyond the 80C limit.
   - Employer contribution deductible under Section 80CCD(2).
4. **Maturity at 60**: Up to 60% can be withdrawn as a 100% tax-free lump sum; remaining 40% is mandatorily annuitized to provide monthly pension.

## Ideal Role:
- Long-term retirement compounding vehicle for young earners seeking low-cost disciplined retirement accumulation.
