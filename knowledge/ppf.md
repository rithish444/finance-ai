# Public Provident Fund (PPF)

## What is PPF?
The Public Provident Fund (PPF) is a central government-backed long-term savings scheme designed to cultivate disciplined retirement and long-term capital accumulation for Indian citizens.

## Key Attributes:
1. **Sovereign Guarantee**: 100% principal and interest guaranteed by the Government of India with zero credit risk.
2. **EEE Tax Status**:
   - Exempt on contribution (under Section 80C up to ₹1.5 lakh/year in the Old Tax Regime).
   - Exempt on annual interest accumulation.
   - Exempt on maturity proceeds.
3. **Lock-in Period**: 15 financial years, with partial withdrawal and loan facilities permitted starting from the 7th year. Can be extended in 5-year blocks indefinitely.
4. **Current Benchmark Interest**: Typically 7.1% compounded annually (notified quarterly by Ministry of Finance).

## Ideal Role in Portfolio:
- The risk-free debt anchor for 15+ year goals (e.g., child higher education, retirement foundation).
- Provides psychological calm alongside volatile equity index SIPs.
