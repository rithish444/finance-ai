# ASSUMPTIONS.md

Every decision made autonomously on behalf of an Indian first-time investor:

1. **Emergency Fund Sizing**: Calculated at 6 months of essential survival expenses for single earners, 9 months if family dependents > 0, and 12 months for variable/business income, because sudden medical or employment disruptions in India require stronger cushions in the absence of broad state welfare.
2. **Monthly Essential Burn Definition**: Defined as Fixed Obligations + 50% of Variable Expenses (not 100% of variable), because in a genuine crisis, discretionary dining, shopping, and entertainment can be immediately halved.
3. **High-Interest Debt Threshold (10% APR)**: Any debt with an interest rate ≥ 10.0% (e.g. credit cards at 36–42%, personal loans at 12–18%) is classified as high-priority debt requiring immediate prepayment before investing in equities, because paying off a 38% credit card provides a guaranteed risk-free 38% return that outperforms any equity market.
4. **Debt Repayment Strategy**: Implemented the **Debt Avalanche** method (highest interest rate first) as the default recommendation, because it minimizes total interest paid to banks and maximizes mathematical wealth recovery.
5. **Goal Asset Class Allocation Horizons**:
   - `< 1 year`: Overnight / Liquid Mutual Funds, auto-sweep savings accounts, and short FDs (capital preservation, zero drawdown risk).
   - `1 to 3 years`: Bank FDs, Recurring Deposits, and short/low duration debt funds (predictable return, safe against rate cycles).
   - `3 to 5 years`: Balanced Hybrid & Dynamic Asset Allocation funds (moderate equity participation with debt downside cushion).
   - `5+ years`: Broad-market index fund SIPs (Nifty 50) combined with PPF/NPS (inflation-beating long-term equity compounding).
6. **No Individual Stocks or Cryptocurrencies**: Strictly restricted recommendations to broad SEBI-regulated asset categories, because over 75% of Indian retail stock pickers lose money or underperform the Nifty 50 over 5 years.
7. **Real Estate Guidance**: Explicitly advised against purchasing direct physical land/apartments for beginner and low-to-moderate monthly earners, because physical property requires large upfront capital, incurs 5–7% illiquid transaction costs, and cannot be partially liquidated during emergencies. Introduced fractional REITs as a liquid alternative.
8. **Gold Allocation Cap**: Capped gold recommendation strictly at 5% to 10% of total portfolio via Sovereign Gold Bonds (SGB) or Gold ETFs rather than physical jewelry, because gold acts as a currency/crisis hedge, not a high-growth wealth compounding engine, and physical jewelry loses 10–25% to making charges.
9. **SIP Timing Logic**: Scheduled automated SIPs exactly 2–3 days after the user's monthly salary credit day (or default 5th of month), ensuring savings are locked before lifestyle expenses drain the account.
10. **Near-Term De-risking Trigger**: When any goal is within 12 months of its target date, recommended shifting funds into short FDs or liquid funds to eliminate market correction risks right before the expenditure.
11. **Financial Health Score Weights**: Allocated 30 pts to Savings Rate, 25 pts to Emergency Cushion, 25 pts to Debt Load, and 20 pts to Goal Feasibility, prioritizing survivability and debt freedom over speculative investment targets.
12. **Assumed Long-Term Returns**:
    - Fixed Deposit: 7.0% p.a.
    - Index Fund SIP (Nifty 50): 12.0% p.a.
    - Gold (SGB/ETF): 9.0% p.a.
    - Inflation: 6.0% p.a.
    These numbers are transparently labeled as conservative planning assumptions, not live or guaranteed rates.
13. **Deterministic AI Fallback**: If no LLM API key (`GEMINI_API_KEY`) is set or if network calls are blocked, the app automatically switches to rich, grounded deterministic template explanations so that judges and users experience a seamless demo that never crashes.
14. **Multilingual Text Rendering**: The user can switch between English, Hindi, and Tamil; mathematical numbers and calculations remain strictly identical while labels, explanations, and advice adapt.
15. **Privacy and Zero-Storage Architecture**: All computations are performed strictly in-memory within the local Python runtime without persisting or transmitting financial data to external databases.
